# OGUN STATE ELECTRICITY REGULATORY COMMISSION

## REGISTRATION FORM SELECTION GUIDE

*Which form to file under the OGSERC Commencement and Registration Order, 2026 (Schedule 3 categories)*

| Cat. | Who registers | Form | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Successor distribution companies, including a multi-State licensee that is a successor distribution company, for all their activities in Ogun State | REG-01A | ₦1,000,000 |
| B | Integrated utilities, for all their activities in Ogun State | REG-01B | ₦1,000,000 |
| C | Generation of 10 MW or more, whether for sale or captive use | REG-01C | ₦500,000 |
| D | Generation of 1 MW to less than 10 MW, whether for sale or captive use | REG-01D | ₦300,000 |
| E | Mini-grids and interconnected mini-grids | REG-01E | ₦250,000 |
| F | Distribution licensees other than successor distribution companies; trading and bulk supply | REG-01F | ₦500,000 |
| G | Private, estate and industrial-park networks | REG-01G | ₦500,000 |
| H | Metering service providers | REG-01H | ₦250,000 |
| I | Own-use generation notification under paragraph 3(4) (200 kW to below 1 MW) | REG-01I | ₦100,000 |
| J | Franchisees and sub-franchisees | REG-01J | ₦250,000 |
| K | Any other activity below 1 MW | REG-01K | ₦100,000 |

### Rules for choosing

- One registration covers all your activities and locations in Ogun State (para 11(4)).
- If you fall into more than one category, file the form for the highest category and tick your other activities; the fee for the highest category applies (para 17(1)).
- A successor distribution company always files REG-01A, and an integrated utility REG-01B, for all their activities in Ogun State (para 17(1)).
- Own-use generation below 200 kW: nothing to file. 200 kW to below 1 MW: REG-01I. 1 MW or more: REG-01D or REG-01C, even if used only on site.
- Late registration: Categories A, B, C, F and G — ₦1,000,000 plus ₦200,000 a day (cap ₦20m); D, E, H and J — ₦500,000 plus ₦100,000 a day (cap ₦10m); K — ₦100,000 plus ₦10,000 a day (cap ₦2m) (Schedule 3, Part B).
